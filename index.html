FAQ

Questions, answered.

Don’t see yours? Text us at (919) 632-3198 and we’ll get right back to you.

Do you really come to me?

Yes. We detail at your home, apartment, or workplace anywhere in our service area. We just need room to open the doors and work around the vehicle, plus permission to be there if it’s a shared lot.

How accurate is the online estimate?

It’s a rough range based on your vehicle’s size, the package, add-ons, and how dirty it is. Your exact price can land a little higher or lower once we see the vehicle, and we always confirm it with you before we start.

Do I need to provide water or power?

Access to an outdoor spigot and outlet helps. If you don’t have either, mention it in your request and we’ll work out the best option before your appointment.

How long does a detail take?

Most appointments take 2 to 5 hours depending on the package, vehicle size, and condition. We’ll give you a time estimate when we confirm.

Do I need to be there the whole time?

Nope. As long as we can get into the vehicle, you can go about your day. We’ll let you know as soon as it’s finished.

What if the weather is bad?

If rain or cold would compromise the results, we’ll reach out to reschedule at no cost to you.

How do I pay?

You pay once the job is done and you’ve seen the results. We’ll confirm accepted payment methods when you book.
